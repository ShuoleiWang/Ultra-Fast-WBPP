# Light, Flat, Dark, and Bias calibration

Ultra-Fast WBPP accepts raw calibration frames or already integrated masters and pairs them with the Lights by the rules of PixInsight's WBPP. Raw frames are integrated into one master per group: type, size, binning, colour, the filter of a Flat, the exposure of a Dark, the grouping keywords of the path (`NIGHT_1/`, see [Files from PixInsight WBPP](#files-from-pixinsight-wbpp)) and, beyond WBPP, the acquisition settings (camera, gain, offset, readout mode), so that no master mixes settings. Every supplied master is a group of its own. Each Light, and each group of raw Flats, then takes the Bias, Dark and Flat group that fits it best. The desktop standard workflow can omit separate Bias when matching bias-inclusive Darks cover every raw target. `strict-v1` (old command-line recipes) keeps its fail-closed rules: one Bias source, raw and master Flats/Darks only for disjoint filter/exposure profiles, exact Dark exposures and no ambiguous choice.

Within one ordinary single-field E2E run, raw Bias/Dark/Flat masters are built once for registration and then reused by the final pixel pipeline through a private content-bound handoff. This is not the user-supplied-master path: the handoff binds every source, generated master, Dark bias semantics, Flat application scale, and upstream receipt, and fails closed on drift. The raw-rebuild oracle must remain pixel-identical before this optimization is accepted for another execution mode.

For a Dark that includes bias, the Light operation is `Light - MasterDark`; subtracting MasterBias again would double-remove the pedestal. As in WBPP, a group of raw Flats uses a bias-inclusive Dark/Flat-Dark within 0.5 s of its exposure when a Bias exists (the closest Dark when none does), otherwise the matched Bias, before normalization and robust integration; `strict-v1` requires the exact exposure. Darks are never scaled. Every master records input identities, combine/rejection parameters, finite-pixel statistics, normalization, and checksum.

Additive Bias/Dark calibration also requires an explicit numeric-domain contract. Unsigned camera FITS inputs derive their zero-based sensor-code extent from `BITPIX`, `BSCALE`, and `BZERO`; a nonzero physical lower endpoint is currently rejected. Float XISF inputs are accepted as normalized samples only when the XISF image metadata explicitly declares finite `bounds="0:1"` (equivalent decimal spellings are accepted). Thus a normalized PixInsight Float32 MasterDark is multiplied by the Light's sensor-code extent before subtraction, while a raw-built MasterDark already in the Light domain retains application scale 1. The registration preview and full pixel pipeline consume the same resolved scales, and receipts record storage evidence, normalized-unit scale, authority, and each additive application scale. A Float FITS additive master is ambiguous unless its exact SHA-bound override declares `numericDomain` and `normalizedUnitScale`; file extension, dtype, and observed pixel range are never used to guess units.

A supplied MasterBias, MasterDark, or MasterFlat is opened read-only, content-hashed, and used directly. It is never integrated or calibrated a second time. A non-unit MasterFlat is normalized only in the Light application expression, `(Light - calibration) / MasterFlat * robustMedian(MasterFlat)`; the caller's file is unchanged. In the desktop standard workflow, an otherwise undeclared MasterDark follows the usual bias-inclusive convention. Explicit header declarations or a sparse advanced `biasIncluded:false` override identify a bias-subtracted Dark, which also needs a matching Bias. `strict-v1` retains the older explicit-declaration requirement. File hashes remain internal identity checks, not a user confirmation step.

The desktop uses `mono-standard-v1`. Valid roles and image geometry are required, and a Bias, Dark or Flat of another size, binning or colour (mono or a Bayer pattern) never calibrates a Light: when every candidate differs that way, planning stops with `FLAT_INCOMPATIBLE`, `DARK_INCOMPATIBLE` or `BIAS_INCOMPATIBLE`. A Flat needs the Light's filter. Every other difference is paired the way WBPP pairs it, and reported as a warning in the plan and in the run receipt's `calibrationMatching`, which also records every group and the groups each Light was calibrated with:

- Another camera, gain, offset or readout mode: `FLAT_SETTINGS_DIFFER`, `DARK_SETTINGS_DIFFER`, `BIAS_SETTINGS_DIFFER`. A group with the Light's settings is always preferred.
- No Dark of the Light's exposure: the closest one is subtracted unscaled, as WBPP does without dark optimization (`DARK_EXPOSURE_DIFFERS`). The tolerance for the same exposure is `1e-6` s.
- Dark temperature: a known temperature more than 3 °C away gives `DARK_TEMPERATURE_DIFFERS`, and an unrecorded one `DARK_TEMPERATURE_UNRECORDED`. Raw Darks of one exposure form one master whatever their temperature, with `DARK_GROUP_TEMPERATURE_SPREAD` when they are more than 3 °C apart.
- Several equally good candidates: the first by name is used (`FLAT_MATCH_AMBIGUOUS`, `DARK_MATCH_AMBIGUOUS`, `BIAS_MATCH_AMBIGUOUS`). Raw frames come before a supplied master.
- Nothing fits: `DARK_MATCH_MISSING` or `BIAS_MATCH_MISSING`. Raw Flats without any Bias or Dark are integrated uncalibrated (`FLAT_CALIBRATION_MISSING`).
- Lights of one target and filter taken with different settings are stacked together (`LIGHT_SETTINGS_DIFFER`).

A Light without a fitting Flat blocks while the recipe keeps `flat: REQUIRED`, the default and the desktop's setting. With `flat: OPTIONAL` the Light is not flat-fielded and `FLAT_MATCH_MISSING` is a warning. This is a declared processing convention, not proof of unrecorded metadata.

Old CLI recipes default to `strict-v1`, which still requires positive evidence for the full acquisition profile. Select [mono-standard.json](mono-standard.json) with `--recipe` to use desktop conventions.
Lights of the same filter may contain multiple exposure lengths. Each Light uses its own Dark (the exact exposure when one exists) for both registration preview calibration and full-resolution calibration. Before same-filter integration, calibrated pixels are scaled linearly to the shortest admitted exposure in that filter; the receipt records every source exposure, each scale, the reference exposure, and the summed integration time. Single-exposure groups retain scale 1.0.

Calibration is tiled to stay within the detected memory budget. A recipe cannot select a concurrency/tile combination whose estimated peak exceeds the reserved fraction of unified or system memory.

In standard mono, missing Bayer metadata follows the selected monochrome mode; an explicit Bayer pattern (RGGB, BGGR, GRBG, GBRG) selects the one-shot-colour path, in which the master flat is applied with separate scaling factors per colour channel (see [OSC / CFA](osc-cfa.md)). Normal PI XISF masters therefore do not need a per-file form or values copied from Lights. Advanced overrides are sparse: omitted fields do not replace file metadata, and a real numeric zero remains distinct from an unrecorded value. Numeric units are still validated before additive subtraction. See [XISF pixels and LocalNormalization](normalization-and-xisf.md).

## Files from PixInsight WBPP

A folder prepared for WBPP imports as it is, and its calibration is paired as WBPP pairs it.

- **Raw data arranged for WBPP is read from its headers.** Folders such as `DATE_0322/` with Lights plus a WBPP `masterFlat_…_FILTER-R_mono.xisf` import as they are. WBPP's XISF masters (`imageType` and IMAGETYP `Master Flat`, `Master Dark`, `Master Bias`) are used as supplied masters.
- **Frames without IMAGETYP.** When a frame has no IMAGETYP, the folder or file name names it:
  - `Light(s)`, `Flat(s)`, `Dark(s)`, `Bias`/`Biases`, and the spellings `FlatField` and `Science`.
  - `DarkFlats`/`FlatDarks`, and N.I.N.A.'s IMAGETYP `DARKFLAT`, count as Darks. A raw Flat takes a Dark within 0.5 s of its exposure, so a Flat-Dark calibrates the Flats.
  - A frame without a type in its header or path is a Light, as in WBPP, with a `ROLE_DEFAULTED_TO_LIGHT` warning.
- **Smart naming.** What the header lacks, the path supplies, as WBPP reads it: the filter from `FILTER_Ha` (or a bare filter name in the file name), the binning from `BIN-2` or `BINNING_2`, the exposure from `EXPOSURE_300` or `300s`, the Bayer pattern from `BAYERPAT_RGGB`. A value in the header always wins.
- **WBPP's output is not an input.**
  - Lights that PixInsight already calibrated or registered are refused as Lights, with one `PROCESSED_LIGHT` error: import the raw Lights instead. They keep IMAGETYP `LIGHT`, and processing them again would repeat the dark subtraction and flat division. They are recognised by the XISF properties `PCL:Calibration:*` and `PCL:AlignmentMatrix`, or for FITS by WBPP's `calibrated/`/`registered/` folders together with their `_c`/`_r` suffixes.
  - `masterLight` and `LN_Reference` files are products: the command line ignores them with a warning, and the desktop does not import them.
  - Byte-identical copies of a Light are refused (`DUPLICATE_LIGHT`), so no exposure counts twice.
- **Grouping keywords calibrate each night with its own frames.** A folder or file name such as `NIGHT_1`, `DATE_2026-03-22`, `SESSION-2` or `PANEL 3` carries a grouping keyword. The syntax is WBPP's: the name (any case), then `_`, `-` or a space, then the value; an underscore ends the value, and the innermost occurrence wins.
  - Raw frames with different values are integrated into separate masters, for example one MasterFlat per night and filter.
  - A Light is calibrated only with frames that carry no other value: `NIGHT_2` Lights never take `NIGHT_1` Flats. A frame without the keyword fits every value, so a Bias or Dark library outside the night folders serves every night. Among the fitting groups, the one with the most matching keywords wins, so a night's own MasterFlat is preferred to a common one.
  - Keywords are read only below the folder that contains all inputs, so the folder you import from (`Night_Sky/`, `…_night_2/`) is never read as a keyword.
  - `DATE`, `NIGHT`, `SESSION` and `PANEL` are read. `PANEL` also splits the Lights into mosaic panels.
  - Flats of one filter from several nights without keywords are combined into one master, with a `CAPTURE_SESSION_NOT_VERIFIED` warning.
  - A supplied master takes its keywords from its folder or file name (`NIGHT_1/masterFlat_L.xisf`, `masterFlat_L_NIGHT-1.xisf`); one without any fits every night.
- **Not supported yet:** grouping keywords other than those four, WBPP's smart-naming override (the path replacing header values), dark optimization, and DSLR RAW files.
